Audit of the first public site

The first public version of this project (us-wildfires.netlify.app) set a high bar: national totals to 2025, a drought analysis, a map of every fire, and a habit of featuring its own corrections. This version rebuilt each of its analyses from the original sources, replicated the published numbers, and then tested them more strictly. This page lists what held and what changed.
