What the record is, and is not

The FPA FOD compiles fire reports from federal, state and local systems. It is the best national record of where and when fires started, but it is not a census: states joined and left the reporting systems in different years, so counts of fires can jump for reasons that have nothing to do with fire. This site counts fires only inside each state's usable reporting window, uses acres and national totals for trends, and shows a missing or unknown category on every chart instead of dropping it. It has no fire perimeters, no burn severity and no prescribed fire. Data and methods →
