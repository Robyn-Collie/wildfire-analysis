Seasons and causes

Human ignitions are of recorded fires but of acres. Natural ignitions are of fires and of acres. The cause is undetermined for of fires, and that share is never dropped from a chart on this site.

Prevention calendar

When to run which message, and where. People start of fires whose cause is known. Debris and open burning is the most common known human cause in ; in July, equipment and vehicle use takes over ( of known human causes).

Debris burning has a season, and it differs by region

  • Alaska: busiest in May, Jun, Apr; in March and April.
  • Northeast: busiest in Apr, May, Mar; in March and April.
  • Midwest and Plains (Other): busiest in Apr, May, Mar; in March and April.
  • South: busiest in Mar, Feb, Apr; in March and April.
  • West: busiest in May, Jun, Apr; in March and April.

Burn-permit timing and red-flag messaging for debris burning belong in late winter and spring in the South, the Northeast and the Midwest and Plains, and in late spring in the West and Alaska. The Midwest and Plains region is every state not in the other regions, which also puts DC, Delaware and Maryland in it.

4 July

A typical day in late June or mid July brings new fires nationally; 4 July brings and 5 July (means over 1992-2020). New Year's Eve and Day together run the surrounding days.

Only of 4-5 July fires are coded as fireworks and have no recorded cause, so the spike is a better measure of fireworks fires than the cause code is. Texas shows the spike with almost no fires coded as fireworks: its state records rarely carry a general cause.

Seasonality by region

Peak month for fires and for acres: South and ; West and ; Alaska and ; Northeast ; Other (Midwest, Plains) . Nationally, has the most fires and the most acres.

Prevention-calendar reading. Fires in the South peak in February to April (dormant grass, debris burning), in the West in June to August, in Alaska in May to July. A prevention message timed to the region's own peak reaches people in the weeks that matter; a national campaign timed to the Western summer misses the South's season entirely.
