Conservation

The ignition record on its own cannot say what burned or what was lost. Two joins add that: PAD-US 4.1 for the protection status of the land at the ignition point, and ICS-209-PLUS incident reports for structures, evacuations and personnel on the larger fires. Both are joins to points, not to burned areas, and both say so.

Protected land: the PAD-US join

Every ignition point was joined to the PAD-US 4.1 protected-areas layer ( points): fall inside a PAD-US unit, in the non-PAD-US area (predominantly private land) and in no polygon (22,202 of them in Puerto Rico, which the layer does not cover). GAP status runs from 1 (managed for biodiversity, disturbance events allowed to proceed) to 4 (no known mandate for protection). ; .

Outcomes: the ICS-209-PLUS join

The FPA FOD has no losses. ICS-209-PLUS, the compiled incident-status reports, does: FPA FOD records match one of incidents, and matching is common only for fires of 300 acres and more. The outcome sample is incidents (). Cause agrees between the two sources for of incidents where both record one.

What these joins do not do

They attach attributes to the ignition point. A fire that started on private land and burned into a wilderness area is counted as private-land ignition here; area actually burned inside protected units needs fire perimeters (MTBS), which are not yet joined. ICS-209 reports exist for a minority of fires and are filed by incident teams under time pressure, so the outcome fields are incomplete (fill rates are in the ledger, ics.field_fill_rates_sample).
