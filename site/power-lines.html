Power-line ignitions

The record has one utility cause, "power generation, transmission or distribution". Across 1992-2020 it accounts for and : of all fires and of all acres burned.

What this record cannot say. The cause is coded at ignition by the reporting agency, and of all fires have no general cause, so power-line fires are undercounted by an unknown amount. The code does not distinguish vegetation contact from equipment failure, or transmission from distribution. There is no weather in the record, so wind is not examined. Nothing on this page attributes a fire to a named utility.
