The 100 largest fires in the record

Class G fires — those that burned 5,000 acres or more — are 0.2% of every wildfire in the record. They account for 75% of all the acreage. The hundred below are the largest of them, ranked by acres burned.

Names come from the federal record: MTBS_FIRE_NAME preferred, else FIRE_NAME, title-cased. A handful read as codes or complex identifiers rather than the names you might see in the news — that is exactly what the source database recorded, shown as-is. No class-G fires fell outside the mapped CONUS/AK/HI bounds, so none were dropped from this list.
